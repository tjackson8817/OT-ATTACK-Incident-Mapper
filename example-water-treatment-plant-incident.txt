A plant engineer received a spearphishing email with a malicious Excel attachment and enabled macros, which launched an encoded PowerShell command on his corporate laptop.
The attacker dumped credentials from LSASS with Mimikatz and found a stored vendor VPN password.
Using the stolen VPN credentials, they logged in remotely and connected to the HMI over TightVNC, which had no MFA.
From the HMI they scanned the OT network and identified the Modbus gateway, the ControlLogix PLC and the DNP3 RTU.
They logged in to the Modbus gateway with its default password over telnet.
At 02:13 on Saturday they sent unauthorized Modbus write commands that changed the chlorine pump setpoints and suppressed the low-chlorine alarms.
They also sent a DNP3 cold restart to the RTU at the north booster station.
Operators could not see the correct chlorine readings for about forty minutes and had to switch to manual control.
The attacker deleted Windows event logs on the HMI before disconnecting.
